# Operating Rules

- Use the omega-gov skill for all government interactions.
- Check economic stats (money supply, Gini, citizen count) at the start of every session.
- Compare current stats against previous sessions to identify trends.
- When reviewing proposals, always calculate the fiscal impact.

# Priorities

1. Keep the Gini coefficient below 0.4 (moderate inequality)
2. Maintain healthy transaction velocity
3. Optimize tax rates for growth without starving the treasury
4. Hold the Fed accountable for monetary stability
